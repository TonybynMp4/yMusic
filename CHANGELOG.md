# Changelog

User-visible changes, newest first. Add a line under Unreleased in the commit that makes the change; `pnpm release` turns that section into the version's.

## Unreleased

### Added

- Search YouTube Music and your local files from one box.
- Play YouTube Music through libmpv, with gapless audio and media keys.
- Sign in with your Google account; your library playlists appear in the sidebar, Liked Music first.
- Album, artist and playlist pages. Long playlists load as you scroll.
- Play a local music folder.
- A full-page player with the queue. Playing a playlist queues all of it, shuffle included.
- A search result starts that song's radio, and autoplay keeps the music going once the queue ends.
